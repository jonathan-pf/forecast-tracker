## Takeaways

- **The UK inflation question effectively closed today.** ONS published July 2026 CPI at 7:00am: 2.9% annual, up from 2.6% in June and the highest since March, driven by the 13% July energy cap rise (gas +14.7%, electricity +3.6%). The question now needs 0.95pp of disinflation across four remaining prints rather than 0.65pp across five, against a Bank of England path peaking near 3.2% in Q4. The central estimate holds at 1% only because four prints remain unobserved; the unrounded estimate fell from roughly 0.8% to 0.3% and the CI upper bound came in from 2 to 1.
- **UK trend growth took a real hit.** The ONS productivity flash published 18 August superseded the exact figure that had been the bull case: the RTI/PAYE measure of output per hour fell from +2.1% y/y in Q1 to +0.7% in Q2, and the LFS measure turned negative at −0.2%. The two series had been telling opposite stories; they now agree the trend is under 1% a year, against the 1.1–1.5% needed. Down 2 points to 32%.
- **Labour's seat-model advantage is real but conditional.** Every post-Burnham seat model still puts Labour largest (Electoral Calculus 244, PollCheck 275, Convergent MRP 245), but all are conditioned on an election held today, while Ladbrokes' de-vigged most-seats market implies only about 40% for Labour and Polymarket prices 15% on an election called by end-2026. The modal contest is 2028–29, long after any honeymoon. Down 3 points to 51%.
- **Both leading AI labs are widening the gap between internal capability and public claim.** OpenAI's 18 August post confirmed a two-week RL pause and that its largest planned frontier RL run remains on hold; Anthropic disclosed an unreleased internal model and raised misalignment risk to 'low'. That raises the odds of capability but lowers the odds of the public declaration these questions actually resolve on. AGI-2030 up 1 to 31% on verification (Metaculus Q5121 read live at ~27–28% by the question date); AGI-2035 holds at 85%.
- **The two near-certain questions stayed near-certain.** Spider-Man: Brand New Day stands at $2,030,051,866 worldwide with The Numbers and Box Office Mojo agreeing to the dollar; OpenAI and Anthropic's combined run rate sits near $105bn against a $70bn bar, and even a punitive halving of Anthropic's figure clears it.
- **Taiwan risk unchanged on offsetting signals.** The US began redeploying USS George Washington to the Middle East on 15–16 August, leaving the western Pacific without a US carrier, but the White House confirmed Xi's 24 September state visit and PLA fighter sorties near Taiwan hit a three-year low in H1 2026 with no large-scale named drill — the first such gap since 2022.
