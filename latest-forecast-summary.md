## Movements

- **AGI before 2030: 31 to 30.** The 19-21 August 2026 window produced a genuinely busy news tape and one large market repricing, but almost every instrument that prices the actual resolution event - a lab or a group of researchers publicly declaring AGI - either softened or held flat. The single big upward move sits one causal step removed from the question. Net, a one-point move down, and I want to be explicit that this is a marginal call rather than a decisive one.
- **Consultancy -33% by 2030 (AI): 13 to 12.** The 19-21 August 2026 window produced almost nothing new that bears directly on resolution. I ran thirteen distinct searches and fetched five primary or near-primary sources, and the honest summary is that this was a quiet window in which the only genuinely load-bearing item is one I verified myself from official data rather than one that broke in the news. I am therefore making a small downward move rather than a hold, and the reason is arithmetic rather than headline.
- **Labour largest party at next GE: 51 to 49.** Unlike the 18-19 August window, this one produced real fieldwork - four national voting-intention polls whose fieldwork post-dates the Opinium 12-14 August survey that anchored the last two forecasts - and on balance they compress Labour's lead from roughly two to three points down to approximately zero. That is a genuine, dated deterioration, and it is exactly the shape the honeymoon-decay hypothesis in the base rate predicts, so it deserves a small move down rather than the shrug a composition artefact would...

Held: AGI before 2035, China blockade/invasion of Taiwan before 2030, UK CPI below 2.0% before 2027, Spider-Man: BND over $1.5B in 2026, OpenAI + Anthropic ARR over $70B, UK real GDP growth over 1.5% in 2030s.

## Takeaways

- The two moves both came from verified primary data rather than narrative. Labour's two-point cut follows genuine new fieldwork (YouGov 19 Aug showing Reform 24 / Labour 22), the first window in a while to produce any; the AGI-2030 one-point cut follows prediction markets softening across Kalshi, Manifold and Polymarket, plus reversion of a thin 19 Aug print.
- Q3 is now the only question sitting near a coin flip, and it is the one with the most genuine day-to-day information flow. It is worth watching whether the Labour-Reform compression in mid-August fieldwork persists into next week's polls or proves to be noise around a single house effect.
- The consultancy question keeps grinding down as the arithmetic gets harder: BLS consulting employment at 1,886,600 in July 2026 is above the 2024 baseline, so YES now needs roughly a 34% fall against a series whose worst-ever drawdown is 6%.
- Q6 (UK CPI), Q7 (Spider-Man) and Q8 (AI revenue) are effectively resolved in opposite directions and are held at their practical floors and ceilings. They are being carried for record-keeping rather than because there is meaningful uncertainty left.
- The two long-horizon structural questions (AGI 2035, UK GDP in the 2030s) held, correctly - nothing in a two-day window should move an eight-year question.
