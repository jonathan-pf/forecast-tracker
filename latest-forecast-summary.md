## Movements

### Consultancy ≥33% loss by 2030: 22% → 23% (+1pp)

Up 1pp to 23%. Industry coverage in the past 24 hours continues to confirm structural pressure: McKinsey ~10% non-client cuts, BCG growing only via AI/tech specialists rather than generalists, Bain/Deloitte/KPMG/Accenture all in cut mode in 2025–26. The May 4 OpenAI ($4B Deployment Co) and Anthropic ($1.5B JV) consulting ventures remain the structural marker. With ~4.5 years to deadline and the demand-side delivery model now actively contested by capitalised AI-native entrants, drift continues upward. Still well below 50% — incumbents have margins to absorb AI as augmentation rather than substitution and revenue erosion to date is concentrated in headcount, not topline.

### Labour largest at next UK GE: 2% → 1% (-1pp)

Down 1pp to 1%. May 7 local elections now substantially declared (~129 of 136 councils): Reform UK +1,428 seats, Labour −1,375, with Sky News National Equivalent Vote at Reform 27%, Conservative 20%, Labour 15%, Green 14%, LD 14%, Independents 10%. Labour is now the third party on national vote share. Farage declared a 'historic shift'; Starmer says he won't step down. Council control: Labour lost 34, Reform gained 13. For Labour to be largest party at the next GE, the electorate would need to swing ~10pp net back to Labour from Reform/Greens AND for Reform's surge to deflate to a sub-25% share — neither is consistent with current trajectory or with the structural Reform-Conservative right that this NEV implies.
