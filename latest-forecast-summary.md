- **Labour largest party (29% → 35%)** is the big mover: the Electoral Calculus/Find Out Now MRP published 8 July projects Labour largest on 217 seats, and bookmakers have flipped Labour to favourite for most seats ahead of Burnham's uncontested coronation on 17 July.
