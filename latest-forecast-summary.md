## Key movements

**Will Labour be largest party at next UK General Election?** — 7% → 30% (+23pp)
Nudged up 7→30% — a large correction, because the prior chain had not priced in three structural shifts. Starmer announced his resignation on 22 June 2026 and Andy Burnham is set to succeed him (322 MP nominations); Rupert Lowe's Restore Britain is taking ~7% off Reform's right flank, cutting Reform from ~31% in January to ~22%; and the Electoral Calculus/PLMR MRP (fieldwork 23-30 June, published 8 July 2026, n>5,500) now projects Labour as LARGEST PARTY on 217 seats vs Con 151 and Reform 127. Betting markets have flipped with it — Labour is now ~13/8 favourite for most seats (~34% normalised), so the 7% prior sat roughly 25pp below the market. Discounted modestly below market for honeymoon decay.

**Will Spider-Man: Brand New Day gross over $1.5B worldwide by 31 December 2026?** — 94% → 80% (-14pp)
Nudged down 94→80% — a deliberate correction of an over-confident prior. The new evidence is genuinely excellent (Deadline, 8 July: $180m+ domestic opening tracking; Boxoffice Pro long-range $230-250m; record first-day US presales, best since No Way Home; confirmed China release), but it implies a global opening of roughly $500-550m, which still needs a ~2.7-3.0x worldwide multiplier to clear $1.5B. That is attainable with strong legs but not assured: press consensus frames the film as 'all but certain' to pass Deadpool & Wolverine's $1.34B while toppling No Way Home's $1.91B 'feels like a stretch' — a range that straddles the bar.

**Will economic consultancy lose ≥33% value/jobs by end 2030 due to AI?** — 28% → 24% (-4pp)
Nudged down 28→24%. The cleanest sector-specific read is disconfirming: CRA International — a listed pure-play economic consultancy — reported record quarterly revenue of $201.0m in Q1 2026 (+10.5% YoY) with consultant headcount UP 2.5% YoY and FY26 guidance of $785-805m (7 May 2026), while Accenture consulting revenue rose 4% in Q3 FY26. The bearish signals remain real but compositional (McKinsey ~45k→40k since 2023; UK Big Four graduate listings -44% YoY) rather than an aggregate revenue or employment collapse — and with 2026 levels at or above the 2024 baseline, a ≥33% fall now requires roughly -9.5%/yr compounding for four straight years.

## Takeaways

- The largest revision is Labour's odds of being largest party (7% → 30%), a correction of a stale prior: Starmer resigned on 22 June, Burnham is set to succeed him, Restore Britain has split Reform's vote, and the 8 July Electoral Calculus MRP now projects Labour largest on 217 seats. Betting markets price ~34%; we sit modestly below for honeymoon decay.
- Spider-Man: Brand New Day cut 94% → 80%. Tracking is excellent but implies a ~$500-550m global opening, which still needs a ~2.7-3.0x multiplier to clear $1.5B. The prior was over-confident about a bar only ~16 films in history have cleared.
- UK CPI below 2% before 2027 is now effectively dead (1%): the BoE and HMT panel both forecast inflation RISING through H2 2026, with only six qualifying data months left.
- The AI cluster is stable: AGI-by-2030 held at 22%, AGI-by-2035 nudged up to 85% on the removal of the OpenAI-Microsoft AGI clause and visible definitional drift, and the OpenAI+Anthropic ARR question stays at 99% with the threshold already crossed on confirmed figures.
- Consultancy AI disruption eased 28% → 24% on disconfirming sector data (CRA record Q1 2026 revenue, headcount up 2.5% YoY).
