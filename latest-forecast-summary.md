## Movements

**AGI before 2030: 30 to 32.** The main development is a clearer read on where prediction markets sit: Kalshi's KXOAIAGI "before 2030" leg trades at 61-64.5c and an independent Octagon aggregate puts P(before 2030) at 58%, both landing well above the standing 30% estimate. Net +2pp.

**Econ consultancy -33% by 2030 (AI): 7 to 6.** No single new catalyst, but the fuller evidence base tilts slightly bearish-for-YES: Accenture's Q3 bookings miss and record one-day stock drop, and a third year of entry-level salary freezes across MBB, are offset by continued Big Four and BCG revenue/headcount growth and McKinsey's own survey showing only 6% of enterprises see measurable AI EBIT impact. Net -1pp.

**Labour largest party at next UK GE: 53 to 52.** A quiet post-recess week. FPTP seat models still show a clear Labour seat lead from a split right-of-centre vote, but Betfair's implied 40.3% sits well below what those seat models suggest, and topline vote share is a genuine dead heat across the last three YouGov polls. Recalibrating slightly toward the market. Net -1pp.

**AGI before 2035: 84 to 83.** The Goodheart Labs dashboard (re-pulled today, median 2031, 80% CI 2027-2044) implies a lower cumulative probability by January 2035 than the standing 84% once fitted to a lognormal — a calibration correction near the ceiling. Partly offset by OpenAI leadership's AGI-adjacent rhetoric and the disjunctive multi-lab resolution path. Net -1pp.

**China Taiwan blockade/invasion before 2030: 7 to 6.** Continued high-intensity gray-zone pressure (Strait "jurisdiction" claims, Pacific diplomatic squeeze on Taiwan) but no amphibious mobilization signal. New: AEI reporting that PLA leadership remains in disarray (only six full generals publicly visible, 21 theater-command posts vacant), which modestly reduces near-term operational readiness. Net -1pp.

**UK CPI below 2.0% before 2027: 0 to 0.** July 2026 CPI printed 2.9%, re-accelerating from June's 2.6% low on a 14.7% gas-price jump, with the Ofgem cap rising a further 4% from October.
