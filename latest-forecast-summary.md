## Takeaways

**A uniformly deflationary day.** Six of nine questions moved down by exactly one point and three held. Nothing moved more than a point, and no update trigger fired cleanly in the YES direction on any question. The common thread is source re-examination rather than new events: on four separate questions, evidence carried into today's prior turned out on closer reading to be older, weaker or more equivocal than recorded.

**The AGI complex diverged from its own hype cycle.** The near-dated question came down to 31 because the OpenAI-Microsoft AGI clause, carried as an up-factor, is dead: OpenAI's 27 April 2026 "next phase" post confirms revenue share runs through 2030 independent of technology progress, and residual verification now runs through an independent expert panel that raises the declaration bar. Kalshi's KXOAIAGI ladder held its bid repricing but 24-hour turnover collapsed from roughly $14,540 to about $1,330, so the volume leg of the confirmation trigger failed. The 2035 question held at 85 - on a nine-year horizon, a single retraced print and a rhetorical walk-back are not worth a point.

**Two questions are now effectively resolved.** Spider-Man: Brand New Day stands at $2.233bn worldwide as of 28 August, 48.8% clear of the $1.5B bar, with Box Office Mojo and The Numbers agreeing to the dollar. Combined OpenAI and Anthropic run-rate is near $105bn against a $70bn bar; even a 30% gross-to-net haircut on both legs that no analyst actually proposes still yields $73.5bn. Both hold, with residual risk that is adjudication risk rather than substantive risk.

**UK inflation's last live route to YES closed.** Ofgem announced on 26 August that the cap rises 4% for 1 October to 31 December (£1,663 to £1,723), firing the biggest update trigger in reverse and fixing the energy component of the October and November reference months administratively higher. With four prints left and July at 2.9%, this drops to 0.
